1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

This Quality Management Manual describes the quality management system (QMS)
of NovaTech Solutions Ltd. It applies to all operations, products, and services
delivered by the organization.

2. QUALITY POLICY

NovaTech Solutions Ltd. is committed to delivering technology services that
consistently meet or exceed customer requirements. We continuously improve our
processes to maintain the highest standards of quality, reliability, and security.

3. ORGANIZATIONAL RESPONSIBILITIES

- CEO: Accountable for overall quality strategy and resource allocation
- COO: Responsible for QMS implementation and monitoring
- Quality Manager: Manages day-to-day QMS operations and audits
- Team Leads: Responsible for process compliance within their teams

4. QUALITY PROCESSES

4.1 Service Design and Development
All new services undergo design review, prototype testing, and user acceptance
testing before release. Non-conformities are tracked and resolved within 5 days.

4.2 Supplier and Vendor Management
Third-party suppliers are evaluated annually against quality criteria including
on-time delivery, defect rates, and compliance with contractual requirements.

4.3 Internal Audits
Internal quality audits are conducted quarterly. Results are reviewed by senior
management and corrective actions are tracked to closure.

4.4 Customer Satisfaction
Customer satisfaction surveys are conducted after each project delivery. Results
are analyzed and used to drive continuous improvement initiatives.

5. DOCUMENT CONTROL

All quality documents are version-controlled and stored in the company's document
management system. Obsolete documents are removed from circulation within 24 hours.

6. CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT

NovaTech follows the Plan-Do-Check-Act (PDCA) cycle for continuous improvement
of all key business processes.
