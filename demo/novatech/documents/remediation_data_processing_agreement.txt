1. PURPOSE

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") governs the processing of personal data
by NovaTech Solutions Ltd. in connection with services provided to clients.

2. SCOPE OF DATA PROCESSING

NovaTech Solutions Ltd. may process the following categories of data:
- Contact information (names, email addresses, phone numbers)
- Account information and transaction records
- System usage logs and analytics data
- Technical support communications

3. LEGAL BASIS FOR PROCESSING

All data processing activities are performed on the following legal bases:
- Contractual necessity (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR)
- Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR)
- Compliance with legal obligations (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR)

4. DATA SECURITY MEASURES

NovaTech Solutions Ltd. implements the following technical and organizational measures:
- AES-256 encryption for data at rest
- TLS 1.3 for data in transit
- Role-based access control (RBAC)
- Regular security audits and penetration testing
- Incident response plan with 72-hour breach notification

5. DATA RETENTION

Personal data is retained only as long as necessary for the stated purpose,
and in accordance with applicable legal requirements. Standard retention: 7 years.

6. DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS

NovaTech Solutions Ltd. will assist clients in fulfilling data subject rights
requests (access, rectification, erasure, portability) within 30 days.

7. SUB-PROCESSORS

NovaTech Solutions Ltd. maintains an up-to-date list of authorized sub-processors.
Clients will be notified of any changes with 30 days advance notice.

8. GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement is governed by applicable data protection legislation including
GDPR where applicable.
