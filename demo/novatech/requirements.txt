NovaTech Solutions Ltd. — Vendor Certification Requirements

PROGRAM: NovaTech Vendor Certification Program (NVCP)
VERSION: 2025.1
EFFECTIVE DATE: January 1, 2025

All vendors seeking certification must satisfy ALL 12 requirements listed below.
Incomplete submissions will not be processed.

---

SECTION 1: LEGAL AND REGISTRATION

REQ-001: Business Registration Certificate
Vendors must provide a valid, current business registration certificate issued by
a recognized government authority. The certificate must confirm the vendor's legal
name, registration number, and registered business address. The certificate must
not be expired at the time of submission.
Required evidence: Official business registration certificate (PDF)
Priority: HIGH

REQ-002: Tax Compliance Certificate
Vendors must provide a tax compliance certificate issued by the relevant national
tax authority confirming the vendor is compliant with all tax obligations.
The certificate must be dated within the last 12 months.
Required evidence: Tax compliance or good standing certificate from tax authority (PDF)
Priority: HIGH

REQ-003: Company Address Confirmation
The vendor's registered company address as stated in ALL submitted documents must
be consistent. Any discrepancy in the company address across different submitted
documents constitutes a compliance conflict.
Required evidence: Consistent address across all submitted documents
Priority: MEDIUM

---

SECTION 2: FINANCIAL

REQ-004: Financial Statement
Vendors must provide audited or certified financial statements for the most recent
fiscal year. Statements must be signed by a licensed auditor or accountant.
Required evidence: Audited financial statement (PDF)
Priority: HIGH

REQ-005: Bank Reference Letter
Vendors must provide a bank reference letter from their primary banking institution
confirming the account is in good standing and the vendor maintains adequate
financial resources.
Required evidence: Bank reference letter on official bank letterhead (PDF)
Priority: MEDIUM

---

SECTION 3: COMPLIANCE AND INSURANCE

REQ-006: General Liability Insurance Certificate
Vendors must carry and provide proof of general liability insurance with a minimum
coverage of USD 2,000,000 per occurrence. The insurance certificate must name
NovaTech Solutions Ltd. as an additional insured party and must not be expired.
Required evidence: Current insurance certificate of liability (PDF)
Priority: CRITICAL

REQ-007: Data Processing Agreement
Vendors who process, handle, or have access to NovaTech data must execute and
return a signed Data Processing Agreement (DPA). The DPA must be signed by an
authorized representative of the vendor.
Required evidence: Signed Data Processing Agreement (PDF)
Priority: CRITICAL

---

SECTION 4: QUALITY AND OPERATIONS

REQ-008: Quality Management Manual or Policy
Vendors must provide evidence of a documented quality management process, either
a formal Quality Management Manual or a Quality Assurance Policy document.
Required evidence: Quality manual or QA policy document (PDF)
Priority: MEDIUM

REQ-009: Organizational Chart
Vendors must provide a current organizational chart showing the reporting structure,
key management roles, and the designated contact person for NovaTech.
Required evidence: Organizational chart (PDF or TXT)
Priority: LOW

REQ-010: Technical Specifications Document
Vendors must provide a technical specifications document describing their primary
product or service offering, technical capabilities, and delivery methodology.
Required evidence: Technical specifications or capabilities document (PDF)
Priority: MEDIUM

---

SECTION 5: REFERENCES AND HISTORY

REQ-011: Client Reference List
Vendors must provide a list of at least three (3) current or recent clients who
can confirm the vendor's service delivery performance.
Required evidence: Client reference list with contact details (PDF or TXT)
Priority: LOW

REQ-012: Company Profile
Vendors must provide a company profile document summarizing the organization's
history, mission, core services, and key personnel. The company address in the
profile must match all other submitted documents.
Required evidence: Company profile document (PDF)
Priority: LOW
